# Payment, Refunds, and Subscription Policies

This page summarises the local-payment policies for RayuCode subscriptions. The binding legal document is the [RayuCode — Local Payment Terms and Conditions](/terms); if this summary and /terms ever disagree, /terms wins.

- **Document:** RayuCode — Local Payment Terms and Conditions
- **Effective date:** 20 October 2026
- **Version:** 2026-10-20

## Payment and Activation

- Pay using the local payment methods offered at checkout, including KHQR where available.
- A subscription is activated only after RayuCode verifies the payment successfully.
- Pay the amount displayed at checkout. The plan, discounts, and promotional codes are shown before payment confirmation.
- A receipt is issued after successful payment verification.

## Subscription Renewal — No Automatic Renewal

RayuCode subscriptions bought through local payment methods **do not renew automatically**.

- The subscription stays active for the period shown in the receipt or subscription details.
- When it expires, you must renew manually to keep paid benefits.
- To renew, select a plan again on RayuCode and complete a new payment with an available local payment method.
- No automatic payment is started when the subscription expires.
- If you do not renew, paid features may be suspended or unavailable after expiry.
- Cancellation means simply not renewing; there is no auto-charge to cancel.

## Promotional Codes and Discounts

- Promotional codes must be valid and applied during checkout.
- Discounts follow the promotion's own terms, eligibility rules, and expiration date.
- A discount applies only to the transaction where it was accepted unless the promotion says otherwise.
- Unless stated, a promotional price does not guarantee the same price on a future renewal.
- RayuCode may reject invalid, expired, or ineligible codes.

## Credits and Usage

- Credits are granted after successful payment verification, according to the selected plan and receipt.
- Credits are used to access eligible RayuCode AI services and features.
- Credit usage, validity, monthly resets, and expiration follow the credit policy shown by RayuCode. See [Credits and limits](/docs/19-credits-and-limits) and [How tokens are charged](/docs/20-how-token-credit-charge).
- Subscription expiry may affect access to paid features even if unused credits remain.

## Payment Verification and Failed Payments

- A payment is successful only after RayuCode or its payment provider verifies it.
- A screenshot or payment notification alone does not activate a subscription.
- If money was deducted but the subscription is not active, contact support with the receipt number and payment reference.
- Do not make another payment before checking the transaction status or contacting support, where reasonably possible.

## Refunds and Payment Disputes

- Refund requests are reviewed according to RayuCode's published refund policy and applicable law.
- Duplicate payments, incorrectly processed transactions, and verified payment errors may be investigated.
- No fixed refund window is published here: refunds are reviewed case by case. Do not assume any fixed number of days.
- To report a payment issue, contact support and provide the receipt number and payment reference.
- Nothing in the terms removes rights or remedies that cannot legally be excluded under applicable law.

## Receipts and Transaction Records

- Every verified transaction receives a unique receipt number.
- A receipt records transaction details such as the plan, amount, discount, credits granted, payment method, payment reference, and subscription period.
- A receipt records the completed transaction; it does not mean future renewals were scheduled. Renewal is always manual.

## Changes to Plans and Pricing

- The price and benefits for a purchase are those confirmed at checkout, subject to applicable law.
- RayuCode may update plans, features, or prices for future purchases.
- Changes to existing subscriptions are handled according to the applicable terms and law.
- For current plan prices, see [Subscription plans](/docs/18-subscription-plans).
